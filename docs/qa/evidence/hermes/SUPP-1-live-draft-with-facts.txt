## BASED ON

Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 15A (snapshot 2026-07-07) -- "An employee is a casual employee of an employer only if: (a) the employment relationship is characterised by an absence of a firm advance commitment to continuing and indefinite work; and (b) the employee would be entitled to a casual loading or a specific rate of pay for casual employees under the terms of a fair work instrument if the employee were a casual employee, or the employee is entitled to such a loading or rate of pay under the contract of employment."

Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 125B (snapshot 2026-07-07) -- employer must give a Casual Employment Information Statement before, or as soon as practicable after, the employee starts employment as a casual employee.

Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 359B (snapshot 2026-07-07) -- "An employer must not dismiss, or threaten to dismiss, an individual who ... performs particular work for the employer; in order to engage the individual as a casual employee to perform the same, or substantially the same, work."

Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 359C (snapshot 2026-07-07) -- an employer must not knowingly make a false statement to persuade an individual to enter into a contract for casual employment to perform the same work they previously performed other than as a casual.

Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 61 (snapshot 2026-07-07) -- the National Employment Standards include casual employment (Division 4A of Part 2-2), maximum weekly hours, annual leave, personal/carer's leave, compassionate leave, public holidays, superannuation contributions, and notice of termination and redundancy pay.
