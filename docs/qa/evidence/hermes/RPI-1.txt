The casual loading framework is drawn from section 294 of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth), which requires that a national minimum wage order "must set the casual loading for award/agreement free employees" (Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 294(1)(c), snapshot 2026-07-07). Section 295(1)(b) requires that "the casual loading for award/agreement free employees must be expressed as a percentage" (Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 295(1)(b), snapshot 2026-07-07).

## 6. CASUAL CONVERSION

Section 15A(5) of the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) provides: "A person who commences employment as a casual employee within the meaning of subsections (1) to (4) remains a casual employee of the employer until (a) the employee's employment status is changed to full-time employment or part-time employment under Division 4A of Part 22" (Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 15A(5)(a), snapshot 2026-07-07). The National Employment Standards under section 61(2)(ba) extend to "casual employment (Division 4A)" (Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 61(2)(ba), snapshot 2026-07-07).
