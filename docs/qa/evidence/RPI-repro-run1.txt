RESEARCH — employment

The Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) does not set a separate notice period for redundancy. The same minimum notice period applies to all terminations, including redundancy. That minimum is in section 117.

---

**Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 117 (snapshot 2026-07-07)**

Section 117(2) states:

"*The employer must not terminate the employee's employment unless: (a) the time between giving the notice and the day of the termination is at least the period (the minimum period of notice) worked out under subsection (3); or (b) the employer has paid to the employee … payment in lieu of notice …*"

Section 117(3) works out the minimum period:

| Employee's period of continuous service | Period |
|---|---|
| Not more than 1 year | 1 week |
| More than 1 year but not more than 3 years | 2 weeks |
| More than 3 years but not more than 5 years | 3 weeks |
| More than 5 years | 4 weeks |

Section 117(3)(b) then adds:

"*then increase the period by 1 week if the employee is over 45 years old and has completed at least 2 years of continuous service with the employer at the end of the day the notice is given.*"

Section 117(4) provides that "*continuous service with the employer does not include periods of employment as a casual employee of the employer.*"

---

**Employees not covered by the notice requirement**

Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 123 (snapshot 2026-07-07) lists employees to whom Subdivision A (notice of termination) does not apply:

- (1)(a) An employee employed for a specified period, task or season
- (1)(b) An employee terminated for serious misconduct
- (1)(c) A casual employee
- (1)(d) Certain employees under a training arrangement
- (3)(b) A daily hire employee in building and construction
- (3)(c) A daily hire employee in the meat industry (slaughter of livestock)
- (3)(d) A weekly hire employee in the meat industry whose termination is determined solely by seasonal factors

---

**Note on redundancy pay (separate entitlement)**

The notice period under s 117 is separate from redundancy pay under s 119. An employee made redundant may be entitled to both: notice (or payment in lieu) under s 117, plus redundancy pay under s 119. Section 119 sets redundancy pay from 4 weeks (1-2 years service) up to 16 weeks (9-10 years service), dropping to 12 weeks at 10+ years.
