The Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) does not prescribe a separate notice period that applies only to redundancy. The notice period for a redundancy termination is the same minimum notice period that applies to any termination of employment, set out in section 117.

**Minimum notice period — Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 117(2)–(3) (snapshot 2026-07-07)**

Section 117(2) requires that the employer must not terminate unless:

> "(a) the time between giving the notice and the day of the termination is at least the period (the minimum period of notice) worked out under subsection (3); or
> (b) the employer has paid to the employee … payment in lieu of notice …"

Section 117(3) sets out the minimum period:

> "(a) first, work out the period using the following table:
>
> | Employee's period of continuous service | Period |
> |---|---|
> | Not more than 1 year | 1 week |
> | More than 1 year but not more than 3 years | 2 weeks |
> | More than 3 years but not more than 5 years | 3 weeks |
> | More than 5 years | 4 weeks |
>
> (b) then increase the period by 1 week if the employee is over 45 years old and has completed at least 2 years of continuous service with the employer at the end of the day the notice is given."

Section 117(4) states that "continuous service with the employer does not include periods of employment as a casual employee."

**Employees not covered — Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 123 (snapshot 2026-07-07)**

Section 123(1) excludes certain employees from the notice provisions (Subdivision A, which includes s 117):

> "(a) an employee employed for a specified period of time, for a specified task, or for the duration of a specified season;
> (b) an employee whose employment is terminated because of serious misconduct;
> (c) a casual employee;
> (d) an employee (other than an apprentice) to whom a training arrangement applies …"

**Key distinction — notice vs redundancy pay**

The notice period (s 117) and redundancy pay (s 119) are separate entitlements. The notice period is the time between giving notice and the day of termination. Redundancy pay under s 119 is an additional lump sum payment based on years of continuous service, ranging from 4 weeks (1–2 years of service) to 12 weeks (10+ years). Both may be payable on a redundancy, provided the employee is not excluded by s 123.

**VERIFY BEFORE USE**
