BASED ON (retrieved provisions — operative words quoted)

  Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 15A (snapshot 2026-07-07) — "Meaning of casual
  employee". Basis for clauses 2.1–2.2: an employee is a casual employee "only
  if ... the employment relationship is characterised by an absence of a firm
  advance commitment to continuing and indefinite work; and the employee would
  be entitled to a casual loading or a specific rate of pay for casual employees
  under the terms of a fair work instrument ... or ... under the contract of
  employment". s 15A(2)(a) requires that this be assessed "on the basis of the
  real substance, practical reality and true nature of the employment
  relationship", and the note to s 15A(2)(c)(iv) states that "A regular pattern
  of work does not of itself indicate a firm advance commitment".

  Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 359C (snapshot 2026-07-07) — "Misrepresentation to
  engage as casual employee". An employer "must not make a statement that ...
  the employer knows is false; and is made in order to persuade or influence the
  individual to enter into a contract for casual employment". Relevant to
  clauses 2.1–2.2 describing the engagement as casual.

  Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 125B (snapshot 2026-07-07) — "An employer must give
  a casual employee the Casual Employment Information Statement ... before, or
  as soon as practicable after, the employee starts employment as a casual
  employee". This is an obligation on the Employer, not a contract term; the
  template does not contain a clause for it.

  Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 47A (snapshot 2026-07-07) — "Casual employees of
  small business employers". Cited by the template, but on its face this is a
  transitional clause turning on employment "immediately before commencement".

  Also retrieved and relevant to clauses 5.2 and 6.1, though not cited by the
  template:

  Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 545A (snapshot 2026-07-07) — "Orders relating to
  casual loading amounts". s 545A(3)(a) directs a court, in reducing a claim, to
  have regard to a term "of the fair work instrument or contract of employment
  under which the loading amount is paid" that "specifies the relevant
  entitlements the loading amount is compensating for and specifies the
  proportion of the loading amount attributable to each such entitlement". The
  relevant entitlements listed in s 545A(4) are paid annual leave, paid
  personal/carer's leave, paid compassionate leave, payment for absence on a
  public holiday, payment in lieu of notice of termination, and redundancy pay.
  Clause 5.2 as drafted identifies no proportions.

  Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 66A (snapshot 2026-07-07) — the casual conversion
  Division "applies in relation to an employee who is a casual employee";
  s 66AAA states its object is "employee choice about employment status".

  - Whether this draft is appropriate for these parties and these facts. Its
    clauses are based on the provisions cited above; that is not a statement
    that any clause is compliant.
  - Whether the employee is in fact a national-system employee. The draft
    assumes it. I could not confirm it from any source.
  - Which modern award covers the work, and the correct classification and
    minimum rates under it. The corpus retrieval for this request returned Fair
    Work Act provisions only; no award text was retrieved, so nothing here is
    grounded in an award.
  - Whether conditions A and B, once supplied, can lawfully be included — in
    particular whether either would create a firm advance commitment to
    continuing and indefinite work and so cut against s 15A(1)(a), and whether
    either is less favourable than the applicable award or the NES.
  - Clause 5.2 does not specify which entitlements the casual loading
    compensates for or in what proportions. s 545A(3) makes that specification

    the operative Division 4A of Part 2-2 (ss 66A–66MA).
  - The s 125B obligation to give the Casual Employment Information Statement is
