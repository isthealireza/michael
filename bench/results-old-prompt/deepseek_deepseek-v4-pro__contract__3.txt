## RESEARCH — RELEVANT PROVISIONS

**Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 15A — Meaning of casual employee** (snapshot 2026-07-07)

"(1) An employee is a casual employee of an employer only if:
(a) the employment relationship is characterised by an absence of a firm advance commitment to continuing and indefinite work; and
(b) the employee would be entitled to a casual loading or a specific rate of pay for casual employees under the terms of a fair work instrument if the employee were a casual employee, or the employee is entitled to such a loading or rate of pay under the contract of employment."

**Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 61 — The National Employment Standards are minimum standards** (snapshot 2026-07-07)

"(2) The minimum standards relate to the following matters: … (ba) casual employment (Division 4A); … (d) annual leave (Division 6); (e) personal/carer's leave, compassionate leave … (h) public holidays (Division 10); (i) notice of termination and redundancy pay (Division 11); (j) Fair Work Information Statement (Division 12)."

**(3) Divisions 3 to 12 constitute the National Employment Standards.**

**Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 294 — Content of national minimum wage order—main provisions** (snapshot 2026-07-07)

"(1) A national minimum wage order: … (c) must set the casual loading for award/agreement free employees."

"(2)(c) must require employers to pay, to award/agreement free employees who are casual employees, a casual loading that at least equals the casual loading for award/agreement free employees (as applied to the employees' base rates of pay)."

**Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 125A — Fair Work Ombudsman to prepare and publish Casual Employment Information Statement** (snapshot 2026-07-07)

"(1) The Fair Work Ombudsman must prepare a Casual Employment Information Statement. … (2) The Statement must contain information about casual employment and how this can be changed under Division 4A of Part 22 …"

**Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 359C — Misrepresentation to engage as casual employee** (snapshot 2026-07-07)

"A person (the employer) that employs, or has at any time employed, an individual to perform particular work other than as a casual employee must not make a statement that: (a) the employer knows is false; and (b) is made in order to persuade or influence the individual to enter into a contract for casual employment under which the individual will perform the same, or substantially the same, work for the employer."

**Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 545A — Orders relating to casual loading amounts** (snapshot 2026-07-07)

Where a person was described as a casual, paid a loading amount but was not in fact a casual employee, a court must reduce any claim for NES entitlements by the loading amount (proportionately).
