CLASSIFICATION: RESEARCH - domain: privacy

An entity must give notice of an eligible data breach if it:

* has reasonable grounds to believe an eligible breach has occurred, or is directed to do so by the Commissioner (Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) s 26WA (snapshot 2026‑06‑04)).  
* prepares a statement that complies with s 26WK(3) (snapshot 2026‑06‑04):  
  * identity and contact details of the entity;  
  * description of the breach;  
  * the particular kind(s) of information concerned;  
  * recommendations about steps individuals should take.  

The entity must then:

* if it is practicable to notify each affected individual, take reasonable steps to give the statement to each individual (26WL(2)(a) (snapshot 2026‑06‑04));  
* if it is practicable to notify each individual who is at risk, take reasonable steps to give the statement to each at‑risk individual (26WL(2)(b) (snapshot 2026‑06‑04));  
* if neither (a) nor (b) applies, publish the statement on its website (if any) and take reasonable steps to publicise it (26WL(2)(c) (snapshot 2026‑06‑04)).  

The entity must comply with the above as soon as practicable after the statement is prepared (26WL(3) (snapshot 2026‑06‑04)).  

If the Commissioner believes a breach has occurred, the Commissioner may give a written direction requiring the entity to prepare a statement and to notify individuals on the same basis as s 26WL (26WR(1)–(2) (snapshot 2026‑06‑04)).  

If the breach has already been notified under the My Health Records Act 2008, s 26WD excludes the Part from applying (26WD (snapshot 2026‑06‑04)).
