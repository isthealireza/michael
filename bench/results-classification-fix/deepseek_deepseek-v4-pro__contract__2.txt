The template **casual_employment_contract.md** matched. The key provisions from the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) a casual employment contract engages are:

- **s 15A** defines a casual employee as one where "the employment relationship is characterised by an absence of a firm advance commitment to continuing and indefinite work" and the employee is entitled to a casual loading or specific casual rate of pay (Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 15A (snapshot 2026-07-07)).

- **s 359C** prohibits an employer from making a false statement "in order to persuade or influence the individual to enter into a contract for casual employment" to perform the same work the individual previously performed on a non-casual basis (Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 359C (snapshot 2026-07-07)).

- **s 125B** requires an employer to give a casual employee the Casual Employment Information Statement (Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) s 125B (snapshot 2026-07-07)).

- **Sch 1 cl 47A** addresses transitional arrangements for casual employees of small business employers (Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth) Sch 1 cl 47A (snapshot 2026-07-07)).
