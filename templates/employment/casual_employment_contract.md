# CASUAL EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT

**Status: DRAFT. Not reviewed. Not executed.**

This contract is drafted on the basis that the employee is a national-system
employee, so that the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth), the National Employment
Standards and the applicable Modern Award govern the employment. It does not
apply Western Australian state employment legislation. Nothing in this document
is a statement that any clause is compliant.

## 2. NATURE OF EMPLOYMENT

2.1 The Employee is engaged as a **casual employee** in the position of
{{POSITION_TITLE}}, commencing on {{COMMENCEMENT_DATE}}.

2.2 There is no firm advance commitment to continuing and indefinite work
